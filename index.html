Acne & Scars

Acne & Acne Scar Treatment

Every plan begins with a dermatologist consultation.

  • Chemical Peels

    Clears the pore and fades the marks acne leaves behind.

  • GFC PRP

    Growth factors from your own blood, used to support scar repair.

  • Microneedling

    Controlled micro-injury that prompts the skin to rebuild.

  • Subcision

    Releases scars tethered from underneath so they can lift.

  • Intralesional Injections

    Flattens raised lumps and nodules within days.

Pigmentation
