Why Lumora

Every Dark Spot Has a Name and the Name Decides the Treatment

Pigmentation is not one condition. Tan, melasma, post-acne marks and sun damage look alike in the mirror and respond to entirely different treatments. A laser that clears one can darken another. At Lumora a dermatologist examines your skin and identifies the pigment before a treatment is chosen, which is why the plan you leave with is yours and not a package sold at a counter.
